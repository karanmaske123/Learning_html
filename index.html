15 AUGUST

15th August is celebrated as **Independence Day in India**. On this day in 1947, India became independent from British rule. We celebrate this special day by hoisting the national flag, singing the national anthem, and remembering the freedom fighters who sacrificed their lives for our country. Independence Day reminds us to love our nation and work together to make India strong and prosperous.

15th August is a proud and important day for every Indian. It reminds us of the courage and sacrifices of our freedom fighters who fought for India's independence. Schools and colleges celebrate the day with flag hoisting, patriotic songs, speeches, and cultural programs. We should respect our country, follow our duties, and always be proud to be Indians.
