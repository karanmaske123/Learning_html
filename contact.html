To reach Annapurna Base Camp, trekkers usually start their journey from Kathmandu, the capital of Nepal. From Kathmandu, they travel to Pokhara by bus or flight. From Pokhara, the journey continues by road to a trekking starting point such as Ghandruk or Jhinu Danda. From there, trekkers walk through villages, forests, and mountain trails for several days until they reach Annapurna Base Camp at an altitude of about 4,130 meters.
